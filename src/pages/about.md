I am Sam Dalgleish, a security researcher and SOC engineer based in Scotland. I hold a first-class BSc (Hons) in Digital Security and Forensics from Glasgow Caledonian University.

My day-to-day interests sit where evidence meets action: incident investigation, endpoint and network telemetry, vulnerability research, reverse engineering, threat intelligence and defensive tooling.

## Published work

I reported CVE-2025-9548, a null pointer dereference in Lenovo's Windows power management driver. Lenovo fixed the issue and credited me in advisory LEN-203293.

I also reported unsafe release entitlements in notarised balenaEtcher builds for macOS. balena corrected the signing configuration in version 2.1.7 and published GHSA-2hc8-w9rq-wff2.

## How I publish

Research on this site is independent work carried out in my own time. It does not represent an employer and does not use employer or customer data.

I try to keep three things clear:

1. What I observed directly.
2. What I infer from those observations.
3. What remains unknown.

That sounds basic, but it prevents an interesting lead from becoming an unsupported claim.
