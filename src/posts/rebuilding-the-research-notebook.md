The old site began as a place to publish threat-intelligence reports. It became too easy for a dated observation to look like a current assessment, and too difficult to distinguish original evidence from a summary of somebody else's reporting.

I rebuilt it as my personal security research notebook. Finished investigations live under Reports. Vulnerability research has its own record. I can also publish shorter technical notes without forcing them into the shape of a formal intelligence product.

## What will be different

- Reports will state the observation period, source material, limitations and last review date.
- Old indicators will be described as historical unless they have been revalidated.
- Threat-actor claims will be labelled as claims.
- Corrections will remain visible.
- Live reference data will come from named public sources rather than being copied into a page and forgotten.

The rebuilt site opens with two pieces of vendor-confirmed vulnerability research: CVE-2025-9548 and the balenaEtcher macOS entitlement finding. I have a HoneyWatch report ready, but I am holding it while I wait for an external publication decision. I have not carried older pages across automatically. They can return after I have checked their evidence and wording again.
